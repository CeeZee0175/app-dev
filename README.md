# My Favorite TV Series

My favorite TV series that improved my character is actually **About Time (2013)**. Its *romance...* at least that's one of its genres. But one with a deeper understanding of it could \*easily*\ say that it is actually a tragedy at its core. The reason why is because the story follows a young man named Tim who discovers that their family has an ability that lets them travel back in time. He eventually uses this ability to pursue the woman he loves, Mary, and as the movie progresses. Mary is about to give birth to their daughter but Tim's sister got into an accident due to her toxic boyfriend. So Tim uses his ability to turn back time to separate his sister and her toxic boyfriend, but Tim soon finds out the consequence of his action as his baby daughter would soon cease to exist and is replaced by a baby boy. Horrified, Tim traveled back in time to undo his actions, eventually fixing his timeline. 

> As such, even the most strangest and strongest ability of all cannot stop what is coming for us in the end, death. That is what makes all the stories interesting and incredible to watch.

The movie progresses once again and Tim has now 2 children. However, Tim's dad gets diagnosed with terminal lung cancer. Tim is devastated but he could do nothing as the cancer started before his children is born. So if Tim travels far back in time again, he would make his children cease to exist and be replaced. So he didn't, instead, he went back and spent time as much as he could with his dad.

As such, the movie talks about the inevitability of life. How even with the most strangest yet strongest ability of all cannot stop what is coming for us in the end, death.[^1] So instead of trying to ~~fight the tide~~, we should instead surrender to it and spend our days to the fullest. That's all :wave:

[^1]: Death is not meant to be a stranger, it sounds menacing but eventually. All things must come to an end... whether we like it or not.

The movie made me run out of H~2~O due to how sad it was. It was X^2^ more sadder than the anime of `Violet Evergarden` but anyways, I still could find it in myself to recommend `About Time` so check it out here. [About Time (2013)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2194499/).

##### As for what Tim learnt
1. Tim learned the inevitability of changing the past
2. Tim learned that spending time with his dad is the best thing he could do despite his ability.
3. Tim accepts that death is inevitable.

- The story starts as romance and ended in tragedy
- The ending is bittersweet.
- The story is deeper than it first appears.

- [x] Romance as its genre
- [x] Comedy as its genre
- [ ] Watch it again
